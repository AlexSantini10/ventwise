## Safe use

VentWise is not a safety, security, weather-protection, or automation-control
system. Do not rely on it to prevent injury, property damage, intrusion, rain
exposure, or equipment damage. Verify actual conditions before acting on a
recommendation, and keep human oversight for any automation that may affect a
window, ventilation system, shutter, or other physical device.

## Reporting defects

Report defects through the project's GitHub issues. Include the VentWise and
Home Assistant versions, clear reproduction steps, expected and actual
behaviour, and sanitized debug logs when relevant. Never publish credentials,
