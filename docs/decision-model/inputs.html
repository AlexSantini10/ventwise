VENTWISE / 02 - INPUTS & QUALITY

Inputs and data quality

Every observation carries a value, canonical unit, source, observed time, received time, validity, freshness, and availability. Optional means "continue without guessing".

Quality lifecycle

Used

The observation is available, valid, fresh, plausible, and used by the engine.

Discarded

The source exists but is invalid, stale, future-dated, non-finite, or outside plausibility bounds.

Awaiting source

The source is not configured or has not provided a value. The engine records this instead of inventing one.

Freshness and plausibility

Indoor and critical weather readings default to a 15-minute freshness window; forecasts default to 90 minutes. The engine rejects temperatures outside -90 to 70 deg C, humidity and rain percentages outside 0-100%, CO2 outside 0-10,000 ppm, and wind outside 0-150 m/s.

Home Assistant adapter mapping

  • Configured entity sources take precedence over provider values.
  • Temperature and wind units are normalized before entering the core.
  • Multiple window contacts aggregate to open when any contact is open, closed when all are closed, and unavailable when the state is mixed/unknown.
  • Forecast values retain a distinct weather.forecast source.
  • Pollen, pollution, and CO2 retain entity provenance in the snapshot and trace.
