VENTWISE / 04 · OPERATIONS

Transformations and delivery

Scoring explains why an action wins; delivery guardrails explain whether that advice is currently emitted.

Derived signals

Apparent temperature

Temperature and relative humidity refine warm-domain comfort. Outside the supported domain, dry-bulb temperature remains available with reduced evidence.

Moisture

Dew point and absolute humidity are derived only from valid temperature and RH. Drier outdoor air can create a targeted ventilation benefit.

Candidate scores

open is sustained thermal benefit; ventilate is transient, moisture, or CO₂ benefit; close is materially worse outdoor air.

Duration

Ventilation is 15 minutes without forecast, 10 with a usable forecast, and 5 with imminent rain or condensation risk.

Trace contract

Traces are ordered and stable. They include the model version, source quality, safety blocks, penalties, score selection, physical adjustments, and delivery suppression. Reason codes remain machine-readable and the result retains both core_action and delivered_action.
