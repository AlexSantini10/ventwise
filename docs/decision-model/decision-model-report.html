VENTWISE / DECISION MODEL EXPLAINABILITY

How VentWise decides

A maintained, practical reference for every data item, parameter, transformation, guardrail, and known scenario in the VentWise decision model.

Model version: 2026-11-11 · Primary source: core models and decision engine · Companion: written Markdown reference

1 Decision at a glance

Core actions

open stable benefit · ventilate bounded temporary benefit · close harmful or unsafe opening · none no meaningful winner.

Safety first

Missing or stale critical weather fails closed for opening. Severe weather, current precipitation, unsafe wind/gusts, and explicit exposure avoid policies win before comfort scoring.

Delivery is separate

Contacts, stability, cooldown, and quiet hours may suppress delivery, but never rewrite core_action. Urgent close delivery during quiet hours is an explicit opt-in, disabled by default. The result always preserves both core and delivered truth.

2 Exhaustive data inventory

Every observation is retained as value, canonical unit, source identifier, observation time, receipt time, validity, freshness, and availability. The engine records whether each input was used, discarded, or awaiting a source. “Optional” means the engine continues without guessing it.

4 Transformations and scoring

Apparent temperature

Warm-domain heat index uses temperature and relative humidity. Outside the supported warm domain, dry-bulb temperature is used with reduced confidence.

Humidity

Magnus dew point and absolute humidity are derived only from valid temperature and RH. Outdoor absolute humidity lower than indoor can create a moisture benefit.

Comfort distance

Distance outside the target band is computed for indoor and outdoor perceived temperature. Positive thermal benefit means outdoor air is materially closer to target.

Candidate scores

open uses sustained thermal benefit; ventilate uses transient thermal, moisture, or CO2 benefit; close uses outdoor materially worse.

Selection

The top score must reach the meaningful-benefit threshold and beat the next candidate by the winner margin. Otherwise the action is none.

Duration

Ventilation guidance is 15 minutes without forecast, 10 with forecast, and 5 with imminent rain. Condensation risk can cap it at 5 minutes.

7 Limits and non-claims

  • Advisory only: not a medical, safety, security, weather-protection, or regulatory system.
  • CO2 is a ventilation indicator, not a universal indoor-air-quality or health threshold.
  • Room physics are not an exact airflow calibration.
  • Forecast and Open-Meteo/CAMS exposure values remain model data with provenance.
  • Missing data stays missing; no occupancy, airflow, or hazard is invented.
