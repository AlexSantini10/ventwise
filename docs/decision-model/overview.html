VENTWISE / 01 · OVERVIEW

How VentWise decides

The decision engine is local, deterministic, explainable, and advisory. It never controls a physical device and never invents a missing observation.

Decision pipeline

Acquire

Read configured Home Assistant entities and optional provider data.

Normalize

Convert temperature and wind readings to canonical units while retaining source metadata.

Validate

Reject invalid, future, stale, non-finite, or implausible values.

Block safety

Safety hazards win before comfort or CO₂ evidence.

Score & select

Compare sustained, transient, and close candidates using thresholds and a winner margin.

Deliver & explain

Apply delivery-only guardrails and preserve the trace, reason code, and both actions.

Precedence

  1. Missing or stale critical outdoor weather fails closed for opening and ventilation.
  2. Severe weather, active precipitation, unsafe wind or gusts, smoke, and exposure avoid block opening and ventilation.
  3. Exposure reduce and imminent rain penalize candidates.
  4. Thermal comfort, moisture, and CO₂ produce candidate scores.
  5. Forecast and physical context adjust duration or confidence only.
  6. Window contacts, quiet hours, cooldown, and stability affect delivery only.

Output contract

FieldMeaning
core_actionPhysical advice selected by the decision engine.
delivered_actionAdvice remaining after contacts and delivery guardrails.
reason_codeStable machine-readable explanation for the selected core outcome.
traceOrdered evidence, block, penalty, selection, and suppression entries.
Bidirectional behavior: a cold room can open toward warmer outdoor air; a hot room can open toward cooler outdoor air. If the outdoor endpoint is useful only temporarily, the action is bounded ventilate.

Limits

CO₂ is a ventilation indicator, not a universal health threshold. Room physics are not calibrated airflow. Forecast and exposure readings may be model data. The integration is not a safety, security, medical, or regulatory system.
